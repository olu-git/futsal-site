## Administrator workflows

- `/admin/login/` uses the browser Supabase client (`src/lib/supabase/browser.ts`) and `is_fis_admin()`; admin route guards are UI controls, not substitutes for grants/RLS. The relevant managers live under `src/app/admin/{results,fixtures,teams,standings,seasons}/`, with reads and mutations under `src/lib/admin/`.
- Results: save draft, submit for review or publish through `publish_result`; a published score is corrected by a superseding draft, not edited in place. Revision history remains available. A snapshot-refresh dispatch failure does not undo publication.
- Fixtures: prepare and validate a versioned change set, review warnings, then publish through the fixture-change RPC. Teams: `save_team_profile` saves core profile, kit, preferences and private note atomically with profile-version stale-edit protection. Standing Adjustments: draft/review/publish signed deltas and use correction/reversal follow-ups. Seasons: create and edit a draft, validate it, then explicitly activate; activation archives matching active editions atomically and creates new season teams without carrying fixtures, results or adjustments.
- `supabase/migrations/` contains installed production migrations; `supabase/production/`, `supabase/verification/` and `supabase/tests/` retain preflight, rollback and concurrency packages. See [supabase/README.md](../supabase/README.md) and the operation-specific runbooks. Do not rerun the completed 2026 import (`supabase/imports/2026-s1/current-season-import.sql`).
